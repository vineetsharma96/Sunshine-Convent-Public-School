OUR STORY / SINCE 2009

A place to learn.
A place to belong.

Sunshine Convent Public School was established in 2009 by Baba Singh Ram Memorial Welfare Society.

Based in Vikas Nagar, New Delhi, we are an independent, CBSE-affiliated secondary school. Our approach brings together academic learning, practical experiences, creative expression, and the values that help children become responsible members of their community.
